Copy this directory shape for a new announcement:

    posts/NN_week-NN/index.qmd

The listing on announcements.qmd picks it up automatically and sorts by date,
newest first — the NN prefix is only to keep the directory readable.

Posts are a dated list of what happens and when. The warmth goes in the Sunday
email (weeklyemail.md at the repo root, not published); the post is the
deadlines pulled out of it.

---
title: "Week N — Mon D to Sun D"
description: "One line, shown under the title in the list."
author: "{{< var instructor.name >}}"
date: 2026-09-14
categories:
  - deadline        # deadline · lab · examlet · logistics · resource
---
