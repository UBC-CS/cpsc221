Welcome to week 3! This is our first examlet week, so there’s a bit more news than usual.

  1. Cinda’s office hours have moved. They’re now Tuesdays, 10:30–11:30a, in ICCS 233. Nathan’s are unchanged: Mondays 4–5p, ICCS X465. Both are on the course website, right at the top.

Week 2 — Mon Sep 14 to Sun Sep 20

Hi everyone,

Welcome to week 2!! We hope your weekend was fantastic.

The news: 1. Labs meet this week, and every week hereafter except for weeks 6, 10, and 14. Attendance at labs is not mandatory, and the material will be available all week, BUT our TAs are amazing and this is your chance to get to know them!

  1. HW1 releases tomorrow night, Due Mon, 09/21, 11:59p. We anticipate that it will take you approximately 3hr. You should engage with the material. We will test your understanding of the concepts via the examlets!

  2. Friday’s class is cancelled…for the rest of the term. Nathan and I will be recording a video of the lesson and posting it by each Thursday morning.(Note that this is new material, just like any class meeting.) Videos will be recorded Wed afternoons at 4:30p. You are welcome to join the zoom! :)

  3. Examlet 1 is next week, self-scheduled in ORCA for some session Thursday (9/24) through Sunday (9/27). It covers all material in the course through week 2 (this week). I will let you know the second the examlet is available for registration on prairietest.com.

  4. To help you prepare for your examlet, we will release practice problems on Wednesday, 09/16, at 11:59p. This will be the pattern throughout the term.

I can’t think of anything else for tonight – you are always welcome to drop us an email (cheeren@cs.ubc.ca, nharms@cs.ubc.ca), or post questions on piazza (https://piazza.com/ubc.ca/winterterm12026/cpsc221). Also, in case you’ve misplaced it, the course website is https://ubc-cs.github.io/cpsc221.

See you in class tomorrow! (Oh, that reminds me of another thing. We know that your decision to attend class is a choice. We make all course materials accessible because we believe it’s the right thing to do, so we know you could glean the material on your own (at 3am?). BUT we think the chances of building your understanding via human communication, and of smiling while you learn, are greatly improved by coming to the class meetings. We love it when you’re in the room…)

All the best! Cinda and Nathan and the CPSC221 teaching team!
