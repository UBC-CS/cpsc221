Subgraph:

\(G'=(V',E')\) is a subgraph of \(G=(V,E)\) iff
\(V'\subseteq V\) and \(E'\subseteq E\) and
\((u,v)\in E'\to u\in V'\) and \(v\in V'\).

 

Degree(v):
number of edges that touch vertex \(v\).
